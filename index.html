(Community)

Events@Rev

A major part of Rev’s mission is to convene the startup community in our region. By providing a space that can act as a hub and home for entrepreneurship events, Rev is more than just a business incubator – we are a destination for discussion, collaboration, and education within the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Our events are designed to bring together the entire community, regardless of affiliation or status. Events at Rev are a great opportunity to sneak a peek at the local startup scene, hear the innovative ideas Ithaca produces, and meet new people – even if you don’t own a business or want to create a startup.

(Why Ithaca)

Ithaca is a top spot for startups

More entrepreneurs are choosing to stay and grow in Ithaca or pick up roots and move to the area. With a wealth of educated and skilled talent, a high quality of life, a vibrant startup scene, and a high volume of issued patents, Ithaca, New York, is a small, tech-savvy community leading the nation.

#1

Best startup spot for tech without the downsides

Bloomberg Businessweek ranked Ithaca the highest out of 300 other U.S. cities for its strong tech hub and high quality of life.

#13

Most innovative city in the US

USA Today and 24/7 Wall Street listed Ithaca #13 on its national list of the “25 Most Innovative Cities.”

#1

Highest in R&D expenditures in NY state

The Southern Tier, the area encompassing Ithaca, NY, saw $1,674 per capita in higher education Research and Development (R&D) expenditures in 2017, greater than any other region in New York State, including NYC.
